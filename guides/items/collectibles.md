# Collectibles

Collectibles are inventory items primarily kept for rarity, collection progress, trading, auctions, or use in another Casino system.

Poker-hand collectibles range from High Card through Royal Flush. Several can also be consumed as temporary multipliers, so check the item description before selling or using one.

Use `/items` for the live rarity and description, `/inventory` to see quantities, and Auctions to trade eligible collectibles with other players.
