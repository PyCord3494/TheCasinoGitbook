# 🎁 Crates

Crates are inventory items containing randomized Casino rewards. Every opening consumes one crate and one universal **Key**.

## Commands

* `/crate list` — compare every crate tier.
* `/crate open <type> [amount]` — open one or more crates.
* `/use Crate` — compatibility shortcut for one Standard crate.

Example: `/crate open Diamond 2`

## Tiers

| Tier | Credits per crate | Diamonds per crate | Item draws |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Standard | Random classic pool | — | Chance-based |
| Silver | 5,000–18,000 | 2–8 | 1 |
| Gold | 15,000–45,000 | 8–20 | 1 |
| Diamond | 40,000–100,000 | 20–55 | 2 |
| Platinum | 100,000–250,000 | 60–150 | 3 |

Higher tiers draw from progressively rarer item pools. Crates themselves are excluded from premium random item draws.

Crate consumption and rewards are processed in one transaction. If you do not own enough crates or Keys, nothing is consumed.

Crates can also be bought with Diamonds where available or traded through Auctions.
