By using the The Casino Discord bot, you agree to be bound by the following Privacy Policy as well as [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy-policy). \
These Policies apply to all users of the Bot, and your continued use signifies your acceptance of these Policies.

The Casino begins storing account data after you use the `/start` command.&#x20;

Your Discord user ID links your casino balances, inventory, progression, game statistics, preferences, rewards, and transaction history to your Discord account.&#x20;

This account data is stored in The Casino's database and is used to provide bot features, preserve progress, prevent duplicate claims, and investigate economy errors or abuse.&#x20;

Discord IDs are publicly available for anyone to retrieve by turning on Discord's Developer Mode.&#x20;

The Casino does not need or request your Discord password, login token, or session credentials. Do not provide those credentials to anyone claiming to represent the bot.&#x20;

Use `/profile view`, `/stats`, `/level`, and `/history` to review major parts of your casino account.&#x20;

If you have privacy questions or want to request removal of your casino account data, join the [support server](https://discord.gg/ggUksVN).&#x20;
